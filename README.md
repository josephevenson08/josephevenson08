## About Me

I’m the kind of developer who likes to understand how things work under the hood. Whether that means a Raspberry Pi automation setup, a small web app, or a more playful project inspired by games I love, I enjoy turning ideas into functioning systems.

My work sits at the intersection of:

- Hardware and embedded/system-level thinking
- Blue-team / security-adjacent networking fundamentals
- Web app development and backend systems
- Building tools that are useful in real life

> I’m not just building for the sake of building — I like projects that teach me something, solve a problem, or make a hobby more fun.

I started leaning harder into projects that match my actual interests, especially the ones that come from real needs or a game I’m actively playing.

| Project | What it is | Stack |

### A little more context on the personal side

- **TunezBot** came out of a real problem in Discord music playback with friends when things just weren’t working how we wanted.
- **ProjectDefeat** is built around the idea of having a single place for all the info I wanted while playing.
- **Illidari Marks** is one of those “I wanted this, so I made it” projects.

That’s honestly the strongest thread in my work: practical + personal + a little weird in a good way.
